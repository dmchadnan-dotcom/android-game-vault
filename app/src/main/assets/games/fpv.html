FLIGHT SCHOOL

Pilot controls

Keyboard: W/S = pitch forward/back · A/D = roll left/right · ↑/↓ = throttle up/down · ←/→ or Q/E = yaw left/right.

Touch (Mode 2): left thumb controls throttle (vertical) and yaw (horizontal); right thumb controls pitch (vertical) and roll (horizontal).

Gamepad: left analog stick throttle/yaw, right analog stick pitch/roll. Standard browser gamepad mapping, dependent on controller.

Shortcuts: C = change camera · M = Angle/Acro · R = reset drone · P/Esc = pause.

Beginner tip: Angle + Hover Aid keeps the drone level and steadies altitude when your throttle is centered. Acro mode uses unrestricted rotation and direct thrust.
