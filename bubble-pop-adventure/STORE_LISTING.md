Aim. Match. POP! Launch into Bubble Pop Adventure, a sparkling offline puzzle experience with colorful bubbles, satisfying combos and charming galactic worlds.

EXPLORE SIX BRIGHT WORLDS
Travel across 60 hand-seeded stages, from the friendly Nebula Nursery to the dazzling Starlight Summit. Each level has its own bubble layout and increasing challenges.

MASTER THE PERFECT SHOT
Aim with a finger, bounce shots off walls, connect three or more same-colored bubbles to pop them, and drop floating clusters for extra points. Chain clever shots to build bigger combos and score stars.

BOOST YOUR WAY TO VICTORY
Use Rainbow bubbles that adapt to nearby colors, Bomb bubbles to clear a nearby area, and a free Swap feature to plan your next shot. Earn virtual coins simply by clearing levels; there are no real-money purchases in this version.

PLAY OFFLINE, YOUR WAY
Enjoy responsive controls, optional game audio, accessible color symbols, high contrast, and reduced animations. No account, internet, or ads are needed for the current release.

• Rainbow and Bomb special shots plus free swaps

• Level unlocks, stars, and locally saved progress
• No ads, account, or in-app purchases in this release
