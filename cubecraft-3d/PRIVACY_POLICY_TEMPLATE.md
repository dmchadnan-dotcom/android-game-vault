CubeCraft 3D runs offline and does not require registration. The initial version does not request Internet, camera, microphone, or location permissions. Cube states, move history, preferences, and timer records are saved only in local application storage (WebView localStorage). These data are not transmitted to the developer by the included code. A user can clear this data in Android Settings > Apps > CubeCraft 3D > Storage > Clear storage; some timer records can also be cleared inside the app.

No advertising, analytics, payments or third-party SDKs are included in the current build. If advertising or analytics are added, this policy and Play Console Data safety answers MUST be updated first. If you provide a support email or external feedback channel, the user data received there is governed by the policy for that channel.
