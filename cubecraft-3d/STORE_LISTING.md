# CubeCraft 3D — Store listing draft

**App title:** CubeCraft 3D: Cube Studio
**Short description:** Twist, scramble and solve a 3D cube. Offline timer, patterns and lessons.

**Full description**

Discover a fresh way to explore colorful twisty puzzles in CubeCraft 3D. Spin a beautifully rendered cube, learn notation and practice turns at your own pace—completely offline.

• Orbit a realistic 3D-style cube using touch gestures
• Practice outer-face moves on 2×2, 3×3, 4×4 or 5×5 puzzle sizes
• Scramble your cube with a tap and undo moves
• Solve any physically valid 3×3 state by entering its six faces, with step-by-step instructions
• Explore eye-catching 3×3 patterns and challenges
• Study eight beginner-oriented lessons
• Improve with a speedcube timer and locally saved records

Note: 2×2, 4×4 and 5×5 solving is limited to reversing moves recorded in the app. Full arbitrary-state solving is available on 3×3 only. Large cubes do not support every slice move. The app does not use a camera scanner.
