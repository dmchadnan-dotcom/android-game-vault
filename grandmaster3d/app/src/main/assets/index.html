How to Play

Tap one of your chess pieces to display legal moves. Tap a highlighted destination to move. Drag the 3D board to orbit the camera and pinch or scroll to zoom. Choose between six AI difficulties or two players sharing one screen. Use Undo, Hint or Switch View anytime.

Chess basics: protect your king; checkmate your opponent's king to win. Special moves include castling, en passant and pawn promotion.
