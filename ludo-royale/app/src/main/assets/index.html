How to play

Objective: Be first to bring all four tokens from base around the board and into the center crown.

Getting started: Roll a 6 to release a token onto your colored starting square. A roll of 6 grants another turn.

Movement: Advance a token clockwise by the exact number rolled. Select any glowing token, or use the large token buttons under the dice.

Capture: Landing on an opponent's token on a non-safe square sends it back to base and awards another roll. Stars and colored starting squares are safe.

Blockades: Two same-color tokens on one track square block opponents from passing or landing there.

Home: Your colored home lane is entered after circling the board. An exact roll is required to reach the crown. Reaching home gives an extra roll.

Three sixes: Rolling three consecutive sixes forfeits the third roll and passes the turn.

Offline only: Computer opponents play on your device; human players take turns on the same device.
