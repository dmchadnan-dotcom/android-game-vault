📘 Mayor's Handbook

Build a balanced city. Paint roads, then construct neighborhoods adjacent to connected roads. Houses need jobs, power, water, and services to thrive. Shops and industry employ citizens but industry creates pollution.

Controls: Tap a tile to build, drag the map to pan, pinch/wheel to zoom. Use the Move tool to inspect freely. Desktop: keys 1–9 pick construction tools, WASD/arrows pan, +/- zoom, Space pause.

Economy: Every few seconds a game month passes. Taxes pay for services, but high taxes decrease happiness. Achieve missions to earn cash and unlock city titles.

Tips: Parks and schools improve your citizens' happiness. A power plant and water tower support growth. Keep industrial buildings away from homes. Game progress saves locally; use Export to back up cities.
