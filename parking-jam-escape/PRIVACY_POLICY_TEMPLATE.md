## Information collected

The game stores your level progress, settings, earned in-game coins, puzzle status, and achievements locally on your device using Android WebView local storage or browser storage. This data is not sent to the publisher or third parties. Uninstalling or clearing app/browser data removes local progress. Your browser or operating system may manage local storage according to its own settings.
