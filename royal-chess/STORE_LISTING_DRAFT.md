# Royal Chess Studio — Store Listing

Name: Royal Chess Studio

Short description: Offline chess with 6 AI levels, clocks, hints and elegant boards.

Full description:
Play chess anywhere with Royal Chess Studio, a polished offline chess experience. Choose one of six computer difficulties, invite a friend for pass-and-play chess, or explore the board in free study mode.

Enjoy three board designs, legal move highlighting, optional clocks, hints, undo, move history, PGN export and FEN position import/export. Standard chess rules are fully supported, including castling, en passant, pawn promotion, checkmate and draw detection.

No Internet connection, subscription, account, or ads required. Learn at your own pace and build confidence one match at a time.
