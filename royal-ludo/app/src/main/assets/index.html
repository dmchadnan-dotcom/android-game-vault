How to Play

Race all four of your tokens around the board and into your colored home lane.

  1. Roll a 6 to place a token onto its starting square. It also awards an extra turn.
  2. Move a token clockwise by exactly the number shown on the die. Select a glowing token when you have multiple moves.
  3. Land on a rival's token to capture it and send it back to base. A capture earns an extra turn.
  4. Starred squares and colored starting squares are safe: tokens on them cannot be captured.
  5. Two of the same color on an ordinary square form a blockade that opponents cannot pass.
  6. Roll three consecutive sixes and your turn is forfeited.
  7. Reach the colored home lane and finish each token with an exact roll. Finishing a token earns an extra turn.
  8. Be the first to bring all four tokens home to win.

House rule: blockades do not apply on safe squares. Online multiplayer, cash prizes, and real-money wagering are not included.
