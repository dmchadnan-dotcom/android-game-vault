Welcome to Snake & Ladders Royale: a charming board-game adventure you can enjoy with family, friends, or computer players — all without needing internet.

Roll your dice, climb golden ladders, avoid colourful snakes, and race to the crown. Play on beautiful hand-crafted boards and try to reach the final square before your opponents.

Discover:
- Classic 100-square Snake & Ladders
- Lightning Sprint: a quick 64-square version
- Adventure Trail: twelve increasingly tricky royal chapters
- Daily Quest: a fresh board based on the date
- Two to four players on one device, including automated opponents
- Optional music-free sound effects and animated token moves
- Offline saved matches and local crowns earned from wins

Everyone uses the same fair dice rules. No account is necessary.

Play at your own pace, pass the device around, and share a timeless race to victory.
