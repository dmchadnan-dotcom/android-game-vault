How to Play

🎲 Roll the die to move your piece forward. Land at the bottom of a ladder to climb; land on a snake's head to slide down. Be the first to reach the crown exactly.

✨ Rolling a six gives you an extra turn. Three consecutive sixes forfeit the third roll. Overshooting the finish means you stay where you are. All players—including bots—use fair random dice.

👑 Play Classic, a quicker Sprint, twelve Adventure chapters, or a Daily Quest. Adventure progress and victories save privately on this device. A saved match resumes automatically.

🔒 No internet, advertising, account, tracking, or real-money purchases are included in this version.
